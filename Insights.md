# 📊 Olist Sales Analysis – Key Insights

## 1. Revenue & Sales Trends
- Total revenue generated is approximately **$15.4M+**, indicating a strong e-commerce presence.
- Sales show **consistent growth over time**, with noticeable spikes during festive and high-demand periods.
- Monthly and quarterly trends reveal **seasonality**, which can be leveraged for marketing and inventory planning.

---

## 2. Orders Overview
- Total orders placed: **~96K+**
- Order volume increased over time, showing **platform growth and customer adoption**.
- Certain months significantly outperform others → opportunity for **seasonal campaign optimization**.

---

## 3. Product Category Performance
- Top-performing categories include:
  - **Bed, Bath & Table**
  - **Health & Beauty**
  - **Sports & Leisure**
- Some categories generate **high volume but low revenue per order**, while others are **low volume but high value**.
- Opportunity:
  - Focus marketing on **high-margin categories**
  - Improve visibility of **low-performing categories**

---

## 4. Average Order Value (AOV)
- Overall AOV: **~150–300 BRL range**
- Higher AOV observed in:
  - Electronics / Computers
  - Premium product categories
- Lower AOV in voucher-based or discounted purchases.
- Insight:
  - Encourage **bundling and upselling strategies** to increase AOV.

---

## 5. Customer Behavior
- Total customers: **~96K**
- Repeat customers: **~2.9% only (~2800 customers)** ❗
- Repeat customers contribute **~5–6% of total revenue**.
- Insight:
  - Customer retention is **very low**
  - Strong need for:
    - Loyalty programs
    - Email marketing
    - Retargeting strategies

---

## 6. Seller Performance
- Active sellers: **~1800+**
- Seller activity has increased over time, indicating **platform trust and growth**.
- Sellers with higher ratings tend to generate **more sales**.
- Insight:
  - Promote top-rated sellers
  - Support low-performing sellers with insights/tools

---

## 7. Ratings & Reviews Impact
- Average customer rating: **~4.1 / 5**
- Products with higher ratings show **better sales performance**.
- Negative reviews often correlate with:
  - Late deliveries
  - Poor product quality
- Insight:
  - Ratings directly impact revenue → focus on **customer satisfaction**

---

## 8. Order Cancellation Analysis
- Cancellation rate: **~0.6% (low)**
- Indicates **efficient order processing system**
- However, cancellations still impact:
  - Seller trust
  - Customer experience

---

## 9. Payment Method Insights
- Most used payment method: **Credit Card**
- Other methods:
  - Boleto
  - Voucher
  - Debit Card
- Credit card users tend to have **higher AOV**
- Insight:
  - Offer incentives on high-value payment methods

---

## 10. Delivery & Logistics Insights
- Late deliveries strongly impact **customer ratings**
- Heavy or high-value orders tend to:
  - Take longer
  - Have higher dissatisfaction risk
- Insight:
  - Improve logistics for:
    - Heavy products
    - Long-distance deliveries

---

## 11. Geographic Insights
- Certain states dominate in:
  - Orders
  - Revenue (e.g., São Paulo)
- Some smaller states show:
  - High AOV but low order volume
- Retention varies significantly across regions.
- Insight:
  - Use **geo-targeted marketing strategies**
  - Expand in high-growth regions

---

## 12. Customer Retention & Growth Opportunity
- ~97% customers are **one-time buyers** ❗
- This is the **biggest business problem**
- Insight:
  - Focus on:
    - Retention strategies
    - Subscription models
    - Personalized recommendations

---

## 🚀 Final Business Recommendations

1. Improve customer retention (highest priority)
2. Focus on high-margin product categories
3. Optimize logistics to reduce delivery delays
4. Leverage high-performing regions for expansion
5. Promote highly rated sellers and products
6. Increase AOV using bundles and upselling
7. Run seasonal campaigns based on sales trends

---

## 📌 Conclusion
The Olist platform shows strong growth and solid fundamentals, but the **major opportunity lies in improving customer retention and operational efficiency**. By focusing on customer experience, logistics, and targeted strategies, Olist can significantly increase long-term profitability.
