# Business Recommendations

Each recommendation states **what, why (linked to an insight), timeframe, and who owns it** — a recommendation without an owner and a timeframe is an observation, not a plan.

## Short-term (0–3 months)

**Launch a targeted repeat-purchase campaign** (email / retargeting) aimed at customers who purchased exactly once in the recent period.
- **Why:** Directly addresses Insight 4 — the lowest-cost, fastest-to-execute lever available, since it targets an existing, already-acquired customer base rather than requiring new spend on acquisition.
- **Owner:** Marketing, in coordination with Customer Success.
- **Success metric:** Repeat Purchase Rate (see `11_KPI_Definitions.md`), tracked monthly from campaign launch.

## Medium-term (3–6 months)

**Build a category-diversification watchlist.**
- **Why:** Addresses Insight 2 — identifies 2–3 adjacent categories with growth potential to reduce Pareto concentration, without abandoning the categories currently driving revenue.
- **Owner:** Sales / Category management, with Supply Chain input on fulfillment feasibility.
- **Success metric:** Category Revenue Share of top-3 categories trending down as a share of total (not in absolute terms).

**Pilot regional marketing spend in one under-penetrated state.**
- **Why:** Addresses Insight 3 — tests directly whether geographic concentration reflects a genuine demand ceiling or an under-invested marketing gap, before committing larger regional expansion budget.
- **Owner:** Marketing, with Finance sign-off on pilot budget.
- **Success metric:** Revenue growth rate in the pilot state vs. a comparable control state over the pilot window.

## Long-term (6–12 months)

**Institutionalize this dashboard and KPI set as the standing monthly business review.**
- **Why:** Addresses the underlying problem stated in `03_Business_Problem.md` — replaces ad hoc, intuition-based reporting with a shared, queryable source of truth across CEO, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Operations, Supply Chain, and Customer Success.
- **Owner:** Analytics function (or whoever inherits this repository), with executive sponsorship to ensure adoption.
- **Success metric:** Monthly review cadence sustained for two consecutive quarters without lapsing.

## Prioritization Logic

Recommendations are ordered by **speed-to-impact vs. cost**, not by insight number: the short-term retention campaign requires no new market entry or headcount and targets an already-acquired customer base, making it the highest-confidence, lowest-risk starting point. The medium-term items require more coordination (category/supply chain, or a funded regional pilot) and are appropriately sequenced after the quick win. The long-term item is process, not a project — it has the least urgency but the highest compounding value if adopted.
