# Conclusion

Olist's revenue base is real, growing at points in the cycle, and built on a workable marketplace model — but it currently carries three structural exposures that are fixable without new capital or new markets: a predictable seasonal ceiling that isn't yet planned around, revenue concentration in a small number of categories and states, and a customer base that isn't compounding through repeat purchases.

This repository traces that finding from raw transaction data (Python cleaning and feature engineering), through SQL-based KPI extraction organized by business module, to an interactive Tableau dashboard, and closes with three prioritized, owned recommendations rather than a list of observations.

**What this project demonstrates technically:** SQL query design around real business questions, Python-based data cleaning and feature engineering, Tableau dashboard construction, and documentation discipline sufficient for another analyst to audit or extend the work.

**What this project demonstrates in business terms:** the ability to move from a dataset to a decision — identifying not just what happened, but what it implies, what it risks, and what a leadership team should do about it in the next two quarters.

**Next steps for this analysis specifically** are documented honestly in `14_Limitations.md` and the README's Future Improvements section — this is deliberately framed as v1 of a standing analytics capability, not a one-time report.
